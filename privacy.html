Privacy Policy for Shuffle

Last updated: 21 October 2026 (music added). This is a courtesy translation; the German version is authoritative.

Controller: Alexander Münd, c/o Block Services, Stuttgarter Str. 106, 70736 Fellbach, Germany; e-mail support@group-o-mat.de

In short: Shuffle has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries. The developer never receives any of your data.

Photo library: with your permission the app reads photos, albums and their metadata (capture time, location, camera details, favourites, ratings, captions, keywords) to display them. It only writes when you trigger it: marking a favourite, changing a rating, or “Also Save to Photos” for a caption. iOS asks for confirmation each time.

On-device processing: face and person detection (Apple Vision) counts people and frames the picture; nobody is identified and the results are not stored. Captions are written by Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models framework) entirely on the device; they are stored in the app's protected container until you delete them in Settings or remove the app.

Maps and places: if a photo has a location, the app sends its coordinates to Apple Maps (MapKit) to look up the place name and show the map. Apple's privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Apple TV and Siri: during Screen Mirroring the app shows photos on your TV via AirPlay. For Siri and Shortcuts the app provides album names to the system.

Music: if you choose music from Apple Music, the app uses Apple's MusicKit with your permission to read your library, search the Apple Music catalog and play the music; Apple's privacy policy applies. If you connect Spotify, the app remote-controls the Spotify app on your device through the Spotify iOS SDK (playback, playlists); Spotify's privacy policy applies. According to Spotify, the SDK collects no data. Only your choice is stored, locally on the device; nothing is sent to the developer.

Settings: your settings are stored locally on the device.

Deletion: “Delete All Captions” in Settings removes stored captions; removing the app deletes all local data. Captions saved to Photos, favourites and ratings stay in your photo library.

Your rights: since the developer receives no personal data, access, rectification and erasure happen directly in the app. Contact the controller above with questions; you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
