Getting started

Allow access to your photos, pick an album and tap Play or Shuffle. Tap a photo in the album to start from there. During the slideshow, tap to show the controls, swipe left or right to skip, swipe down to close, and tap the details card for everything about a photo.

Apple TV

Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your Apple TV. Shuffle shows a layout made for the TV and your iPhone becomes the remote. Turn off “TV Layout” in Settings to mirror the phone instead.

Styles

Choose a look on an album's page under Style, for all other albums in Settings → Standard Style, or while watching in the slideshow's options menu. There you also set the colors, fonts, what is shown, the caption voice, your logo and text line, and an album's opening and closing cards. Photo details, the map, people, weather and banner opacity are part of the style. Portrait pairs appear in Modern and Minimal.

Without an internet connection

Shuffle plays the photos that are on your device, with place names and maps it saved earlier. Photos kept only in iCloud, new places and the weather need a connection.

Captions

Captions are written by Apple Intelligence on your device and need a device that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On an album page, “Prepare” writes captions for every photo ahead of time. Keep Shuffle open while it works (the screen stays on); if you leave the app it pauses and continues when you come back. You can edit, regenerate or remove a caption in a photo's details.

Why aren't people named?

iOS doesn't give apps the names from the People album in Photos. Shuffle detects faces on your device to count people and to focus the Ken Burns effect, but it can't tell who they are.

I only see some of my photos

You may have granted limited access. Change it in Settings → Apps → Shuffle → Photos → Full Access.
