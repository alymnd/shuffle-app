Your albums, beautifully replayed.

Shuffle plays any album from your photo library as a slideshow – at random or in order – and tells the story behind every photo: when, where and who.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS 27 · Coming soon to the App Store

Shuffle or in order

Random, oldest or newest first, album order, or your top-rated photos first.

When, where, who

Capture time in the local time zone, the place on a map with an exact dot, and how many people are in the picture.

Apple Intelligence captions

Short titles written on your device. Prepare an album ahead, edit captions, or save them to Photos.

Made for the big screen

Mirror to an Apple TV and Shuffle switches to a TV layout; your iPhone becomes the remote.

Beautiful motion

Ken Burns that drifts towards faces, dissolve, slide, zoom and dream transitions.

Only the photos you want

Screenshots, receipts and near-identical shots are left out. Rate, favorite or hide a photo while it plays.

Music and weather

A playlist, album or song from Apple Music plays along, and a small icon shows the current weather.

Picture frame

An album shuffles full screen with a clock, goes dark at night and stays on while charging.

Private by design

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your photos never leave your device.
